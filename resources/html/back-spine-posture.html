Cervical & Lumbar Spine Conditions

The spine supports the body, protects the spinal cord and enables movement. The cervical spine refers to the neck region, while the lumbar spine refers to the lower-back region. Conditions affecting these areas may cause pain, stiffness, restricted movement and, in some cases, nerve-related symptoms.

Our spinal health programme provides personalised exercise and nutrition guidance to help individuals build overall strength, improve movement and maintain physical fitness while respecting their diagnosed condition, symptoms and medical recommendations.

Your specialised training includes

  • Controlled Strength Training: Individually adapted resistance exercises to build supporting muscle strength and improve overall physical function.
  • Mobility & Movement Training: Appropriate movement routines to maintain flexibility, improve mobility and support comfortable everyday activities.
  • Nutrition Guidance: Nutritionist-led guidance on balanced macronutrients and micronutrients, portion control and practical meal planning to support overall health.
  • Posture & Lifestyle Tracking: Monitoring daily activity, movement habits, posture awareness, sleep and exercise-related symptoms to support consistent and healthy routines.

Our approach

How we support you

Controlled Strength Training

Individually adapted resistance exercises to build muscle strength, support spinal stability and improve everyday physical function within comfortable movement ranges.

Mobility & Movement Training

Our promise

Safety comes first.

  • We review your spinal condition, medical history, current symptoms, movement limitations and medical recommendations before training.
  • We select appropriate exercises and plan training intensity according to your individual condition, physical capacity and medical clearance.
  • We monitor your response to exercise and modify or stop training if you experience increasing pain, numbness, tingling or unusual discomfort.
  • We encourage regular communication with your treating doctor and follow their recommendations throughout your fitness journey.
  • We refer neurological symptoms, worsening spinal concerns and rehabilitation needs to appropriately qualified healthcare professionals.

Please note: TFI programmes provide fitness and lifestyle support. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, spinal rehabilitation or treatment, and do not replace your doctor or physiotherapist. Always consult your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.

Can I exercise if I have cervical or lumbar spine problems?

Yes, appropriate exercise may be suitable for many people with cervical or lumbar spine conditions. However, exercise selection and intensity depend on your diagnosis, symptoms, physical capacity and medical recommendations. We recommend consulting your doctor and obtaining appropriate medical clearance before starting.

Can exercise cure my neck or lower-back condition?

No. Our programme provides fitness and lifestyle support to help build strength, maintain mobility and improve general physical fitness. It does not cure spinal conditions, correct disc problems or replace medical treatment or rehabilitation.

What happens if I experience pain, numbness or tingling during training?

Your safety comes first. Inform your trainer immediately. Training will be stopped or modified, and medical assessment will be recommended when necessary. New or worsening weakness, difficulty walking or changes in bladder or bowel control require urgent medical attention.

Can I train online?

Yes. We offer online specialised personal training with one-to-one coach guidance, adapted to your fitness level, spinal condition and schedule.

Do I pay straight away?

You can select your programme, choose your training options and view the total cost before committing. Your programme is confirmed after payment.

Can I extend my programme?

Yes. A 10-day extension is available for 1-month programmes, while 3-month and 6-month programmes offer a 30-day extension option. Extensions are available at an additional charge.

Can my partner train with me at the same time?

Yes. Couple training is available, allowing both partners to train together at the same time for an additional fee.
