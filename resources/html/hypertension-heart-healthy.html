Hypertension & Hypotension

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against the walls of the arteries. Hypertension refers to consistently high blood pressure, while hypotension refers to abnormally low blood pressure. Both conditions require individual attention, as they can affect circulation, exercise tolerance and overall wellbeing.

Your specialised training includes

  • Controlled Strength Training: Appropriate resistance exercises to build muscle strength while considering individual blood pressure levels and exercise tolerance.
  • Cardio & Movement Training: Suitable aerobic activities and movement routines to support cardiovascular fitness, circulation and everyday mobility.
  • Nutrition Guidance: Nutritionist-led guidance on balanced macronutrients and micronutrients, portion control and practical meal planning.
  • Health Tracking: Tracking blood pressure readings as advised by your doctor, physical activity, sleep, hydration and exercise-related symptoms to support safe and consistent habits.

Our approach

How we support you

Controlled Strength Training

Gradual resistance exercises with appropriate intensity, breathing techniques and rest periods to support muscle strength and physical fitness.

Cardiovascular Fitness

Individually adapted aerobic activities, warm-ups and cool-downs to improve exercise capacity and support everyday movement.

Balanced Nutrition

Nutritionist-led guidance on balanced macronutrients and micronutrients, portion control and practical meal planning to support overall wellbeing.

Blood Pressure & Habit Tracking

Monitoring blood pressure readings as advised by your doctor, along with physical activity, sleep, hydration and exercise-related symptoms to track progress.

Our promise

Safety comes first.

  • We review your health history, blood pressure condition, medications, symptoms and medical recommendations before training.
  • We plan exercise intensity, session duration and rest periods according to your individual fitness level and medical clearance.
  • We monitor your response to exercise and stop training if you experience warning signs such as dizziness, fainting, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness.
  • We encourage regular communication with your treating doctor and follow their recommendations throughout your fitness journey.

Please note: TFI programmes provide fitness and lifestyle support. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and do not replace your doctor. Always consult your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.

Can I exercise if I have hypertension or hypotension?

Yes, exercise may be suitable for many people with high or low blood pressure. However, your training plan and exercise intensity must be based on your blood pressure levels, symptoms, fitness level and doctor’s recommendations. We recommend medical clearance before starting.

Will this programme replace my blood pressure medication?

No. Our programmes provide fitness and lifestyle support alongside your medical care. We never recommend stopping or changing prescribed medication.

What happens if I experience discomfort during training?

Your safety comes first. Inform your trainer immediately if you experience dizziness, faintness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness. Training will be stopped when necessary, and medical attention will be recommended based on your symptoms.

Can I train online?

Yes. We offer online specialised personal training with one-to-one coach guidance, adapted to your fitness level, health needs and schedule.

Do I pay straight away?

You can select your programme, choose your training options and view the total cost before committing. Your programme is confirmed after payment.

Can I extend my programme?

Yes. A 10-day extension is available for 1-month programmes, while 3-month and 6-month programmes offer a 30-day extension option. Extensions are available at an additional charge.

Can my partner train with me at the same time?

Yes. Couple training is available, allowing both partners to train together at the same time for an additional fee.
